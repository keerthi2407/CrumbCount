
# CrumbCount
### Home Bakery Intelligence System

**Project Type:** Data Analytics & Business Intelligence  
**Domain:** Home Bakery Management  
**Status:** Planning & Research

---

## 1. Project Overview

CrumbCount is a data-driven business intelligence and decision-support system designed for small home bakeries.

The project aims to help home bakers understand and manage their business operations through data analysis. It focuses on recipe costing, pricing, ingredient inventory, sales performance, profitability, batch production and food wastage.

Using Excel, SQL, Power BI and Python, the system will transform bakery operational data into meaningful insights that support informed business decisions.

## 2. Problem Statement

Small home bakers often manage their business operations through manual records or basic spreadsheets, making it difficult to accurately track production costs, manage ingredient inventory, determine appropriate selling prices and understand product-wise profitability.

Additionally, bakers who prepare products in advance may experience unsold inventory, ingredient wastage and financial losses due to uncertain customer demand and limited shelf life.

The absence of an integrated analytical system makes it challenging to evaluate business performance, identify operational inefficiencies and plan future production effectively.

CrumbCount aims to address these challenges by developing a data-driven system that converts bakery operational data into actionable business insights.

## 3. Project Objectives

1. Calculate the actual cost of producing individual bakery products.
2. Analyse selling prices and product profitability.
3. Monitor ingredient purchases, consumption, stock levels and expiry.
4. Identify sales trends, popular products and customer purchasing patterns.
5. Evaluate revenue, costs and overall financial performance.
6. Track advance production, unsold products and food wastage.
7. Explore demand forecasting and future ingredient requirements.
8. Generate actionable insights to support better business decisions.

## 4. Project Scope

CrumbCount will initially support two bakery business models:

### A. Made-to-Order Baking
Products are prepared after receiving customer orders.

### B. Advance-Batch Baking
Products are prepared in advance and sold through channels such as Instagram, WhatsApp and local stalls.

Both models will share costing, inventory and financial analysis capabilities, with additional batch-level tracking for advance production.

### Initial Scope Exclusions
- Online payment processing.
- Customer ordering and delivery applications.
- Automated food-safety certification.
- Full-scale e-commerce functionality.

## 5. Target Users

- Individual home bakers.
- Small home bakery businesses.
- Bakers accepting customised orders.
- Bakers selling ready-made products through social media or local stalls.

## 6. Initial Business Questions

### Costing and Pricing
- What is the actual cost of producing each product?
- Are products being sold at profitable prices?
- How do ingredient price changes affect production costs?

### Inventory Management
- Which ingredients need restocking?
- How much inventory is currently available?
- Which ingredients are at risk of expiring?

### Sales Analysis
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- What are the monthly sales trends?
- Which products have the highest demand?

### Profitability Analysis
- What is the actual financial performance of the bakery?
- Which products contribute most towards profit?
- How do production costs affect profitability?

### Batch and Wastage Analysis
- How much advance-produced inventory remains unsold?
- What is the financial value of wasted products?
- Can production quantities be optimised to reduce wastage?
- How much revenue can be recovered through safe discounted sales?

### Forecasting
- How much should the baker prepare for future demand?
- What quantities of ingredients may be required?
- Can historical sales patterns help improve production planning?

## 8. Analytical Approach

The project will gradually follow these stages:

1. Business understanding and requirement analysis.
2. Data collection and preparation.
3. Exploratory data analysis.
4. Database design and SQL analysis.
5. Data modelling and Power BI dashboard development.
6. Advanced analysis using Python.
7. Interpretation of findings and business recommendations.
8. Testing and continuous improvement.

## 9. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is an analytical decision-support system that helps home bakers understand their operational and financial performance.

The project will also demonstrate practical application of data analytics concepts, database management, business intelligence and analytical problem-solving.
